# nycpluto 0.0.1.9000

* Initial release.

* `nyc_pluto()`, `nyc_pluto_path()`, and `nyc_pluto_to_sf()` give access to
  PLUTO 26v2: all 858,284 NYC tax lots with 97 attribute fields and MapPLUTO
  lot polygons, stored as GeoParquet.

* New datasets `nyc_pluto_changes_df` (the PLUTO change file),
  `nyc_pluto_land_use_df`, and `nyc_pluto_bldg_class_df`.
