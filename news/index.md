# Changelog

## nycpluto 0.0.1.9000

- Initial release.

- [`nyc_pluto()`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycpluto/reference/nyc_pluto.md),
  [`nyc_pluto_path()`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycpluto/reference/nyc_pluto_path.md),
  and
  [`nyc_pluto_to_sf()`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycpluto/reference/nyc_pluto_to_sf.md)
  give access to PLUTO 26v2: all 858,284 NYC tax lots with 97 attribute
  fields and MapPLUTO lot polygons, stored as GeoParquet.

- New datasets `nyc_pluto_changes_df` (the PLUTO change file),
  `nyc_pluto_land_use_df`, and `nyc_pluto_bldg_class_df`.
